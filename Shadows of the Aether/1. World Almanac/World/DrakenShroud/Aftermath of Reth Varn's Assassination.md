# Aftermath of Reth Varn's Assassination

The assassination of [[Reth Varn]] becomes known throughout Drakenshroud as:

# The Black Lantern Murder

Within minutes of Varn's death, bells begin ringing across the city.

The gates close.

Rail service stops.

Iron Cohort soldiers flood the streets.

Nobody knows who is responsible.

Everyone has a theory.

# The Ashen Mandate

Drakenshroud's remaining leadership declares emergency martial law.

The decree becomes known as:

**The Ashen Mandate**

## Restrictions

- Curfew after dusk
- Military checkpoints
- Searches without warrants
- Rail service suspended
- City gates restricted
- Gatherings of more than ten prohibited
- Black Lantern Square under military control
- Increased patrols in Emberforge and The Molt

---

# District Conditions

## Ashward Bastion

**Status:** LOCKDOWN

Military officers argue over responsibility for the assassination.

Prisoners are being moved.

Records are disappearing.

[[Mara Veyne]] is currently held here.

---

## Emberforge Quarter

**Status:** TENSE

Workers are forced to remain at their jobs.

Military production continues despite the assassination.

Resentment is growing.

Graffiti begins appearing:

> **VARN SPOKE. THEY SILENCED HIM.**

[[Lessa Flint]] hears increasingly angry conversations among smiths and workers.

---

## The Molt

**Status:** UNREST

Riots and demonstrations begin.

Smuggling becomes extremely profitable.

Iron Cohort patrols are frequently attacked with bottles, stones, and improvised weapons.

[[Slip Varro]]'s tunnels become extremely valuable.

---

## Cinderveil

**Status:** FEARFUL

Nobles barricade their estates.

Private guards appear everywhere.

Secret political meetings occur nightly.

Everyone is attempting to determine who will replace Varn.

---

## Caldera Sanctum

**Status:** SEALED

The [[Pyrebound Synod]] closes the Sanctum.

Nobody enters.

Nobody leaves.

Each night after Varn's assassination, a low vibration can be felt beneath Drakenshroud.

Most residents assume it is volcanic activity.

It isn't.

---

# NPC Reactions

## [[Hobrin Slate]]

Hobrin notices that some soldiers were prepared for the lockdown unusually quickly.

> "Varn hadn't hit the stones before half the Cohort knew where they were supposed to stand."

He suspects someone planned for the aftermath.

---

## [[Lessa Flint]]

Lessa is furious about forced production quotas.

She begins hearing workers openly discussing resistance.

---

## [[Serrin Sootstitch Vael]]

Business is excellent.

Military uniforms, travel papers, and false identities are suddenly extremely valuable.

> "Amazing what a little political murder does for the tailoring business."

---

## [[Slip Varro]]

The closing of checkpoints makes his tunnels enormously valuable.

His prices triple.

---

## [[Commander Darius Voss]]

Voss becomes increasingly suspicious of his superiors.

He is disturbed by:

- Arrests without charges
- Disappearing prisoners
- Orders to suppress workers
- Evidence being removed from Black Lantern Square

He could become a powerful ally.

# Escalation Clock

Advance the clock whenever the party spends significant time without affecting events.

## Day 0 — Assassination

- Varn dies
- City gates close
- Ashen Mandate declared
- Assassins detained

## Day 1 — The Crackdown

- Mass arrests
- Mara scheduled for transfer
- Synod closes Caldera Sanctum
- Workers begin protesting

## Day 2 — Blood in Emberforge

Iron Cohort soldiers clash with workers.

Someone dies.

Both sides blame the other.

## Day 3 — The First Defection

A small Iron Cohort unit refuses an order.

The soldiers barricade themselves inside a barracks.

## Day 4 — The Spark

Someone attacks an Iron Cohort checkpoint.

Whether it was rebels, cultists, or conspirators is unclear.

## Day 5 — Drakenshroud Fractures

The Iron Cohort begins openly dividing between:

- Crown Loyalists
- Cinderwake Officers
- Ashen Reformers

At this point, civil conflict becomes increasingly difficult to prevent.
