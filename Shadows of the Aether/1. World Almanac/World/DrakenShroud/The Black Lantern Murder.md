# The Black Lantern Murder

> [!summary]
> High Magistrate [[Reth Varn]] has been assassinated during a public address in Black Lantern Square. Three assassins participated. The party killed one, captured [[Tavian Rusk]], and the [[Iron Cohort]] captured [[Mara Veyne]].
>
> The assassination is only one piece of a much larger conspiracy involving [[Project Cinderwake]], the [[Pyrebound Synod]], elements of the Iron Cohort, and something ancient beneath Drakenshroud.

## Current Situation

Reth Varn was assassinated during his public speech in [[Black Lantern Square]].

### The Three Assassins

| Assassin | Role | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Primary Shooter | Kill Reth Varn | Dead |
| [[Tavian Rusk]] | Backup shooter | Captured by party |
| [[Mara Veyne]] | Recover an object from Varn | Captured by Iron Cohort |

The party currently possesses Tavian and intends to interrogate him.

---

# What Really Happened

Reth Varn discovered the existence of [[Project Cinderwake]].

His investigation revealed:

- Secret experiments beneath Drakenshroud
- Cooperation between Iron Cohort officers and radical members of the [[Pyrebound Synod]]
- An ancient Aether structure beneath the mountain
- Evidence that something powerful is sealed within the Caldera
- Connections between this power and unusual Aether phenomena throughout Prythian

Varn intended to use his speech to announce an official investigation into the [[Caldera Sanctum]] and the activities occurring beneath the mountain.

Someone decided he could not be allowed to do so.

> [!important]
> The assassination was not simply about killing Varn.
>
> The conspirators also needed to recover something he possessed.

---

# Act I — Interrogate Tavian Rusk

See: [[Tavian Rusk]]

Tavian is a former Iron Cohort Pathfinder.

He does **not** believe he was working for the Pyrebound Synod.

He was told he was participating in a sanctioned operation against a traitor.

## Tavian's Orders

> "Varn intends to compromise the security of Drakenshroud and expose military assets during his address. He cannot leave Black Lantern Square alive."

Tavian knows that three assassins were assigned different objectives.

### What Tavian Can Reveal

#### Who hired him?

He never saw the handler's face.

He remembers:

- Military cloak
- Officer's posture
- Experienced soldier
- Missing smallest finger on left hand

This description points toward:

**Colonel Garran Volsk**

An Iron Cohort logistics commander.

---

#### What did the cult have to do with it?

> "Didn't work for priests. But they were there. Three red-robed bastards were given access to the square before we arrived. Guards waved them through."

This establishes that someone within the city government or military provided cultists access to the assassination site.

---

#### Why was Varn killed?

> "Our orders called whatever he planned to reveal 'Project Cinderwake.' That's all I know."

See: [[Project Cinderwake]]

---

#### What is beneath the mountain?

If pushed, Tavian admits:

> "I transported prisoners there once. Years ago. Ashward Bastion to a freight lift beneath the old furnaces. Twenty-three people went down."
>
> "We brought four back."

He knows the entrance as:

**Ash Gate Seven**

See: [[Beneath Drakenshroud#Ash Gate Seven]]

---

# Tavian's Hidden Orders

Tavian has a chemically treated scrap of parchment stitched inside his boot.

It reads:

> **CINDERWAKE — PHASE III**
>
> **VARN: TERMINATE**
>
> **VESSEL: RECOVER**
>
> **ASH GATE: PREPARE**

This provides three major leads:

1. [[Project Cinderwake]]
2. The missing "Vessel"
3. [[Beneath Drakenshroud#Ash Gate Seven]]

---

# Act II — The Third Assassin

When Tavian learns the Iron Cohort captured [[Mara Veyne]], he becomes concerned.

> "You let the Cohort take Veyne?"

Mara wasn't primarily assigned to kill Varn.

Her job was to retrieve something from his corpse.

## Mara's Objective

Recover [[Reth Varn]]'s signet ring.

The ring contains or is connected to an Aether resonance Varn discovered during his investigation.

However, when Mara reached Varn:

**the ring was already gone.**

---

# The Race for Mara

Mara is being held inside [[Ashward Bastion]].

Officially, she is being transferred to another facility before dawn.

In reality, she is going to be killed.

The conspirators intend to:

1. Move Mara
2. Ambush the transport
3. Kill her
4. Blame the attack on Synod extremists

## Party Options

The party could:

- Infiltrate Ashward Bastion
- Intercept the convoy
- Follow the transport
- Bribe guards
- Use forged Iron Cohort uniforms
- Contact [[Serrin Sootstitch Vael]]
- Use [[Slip Varro]] to access tunnels beneath the Bastion

---

# Act III — The Missing Ring

If Mara survives, she reveals:

> "I reached Varn."
>
> "The ring wasn't there."

Someone took it during the chaos.

Evidence initially points toward:

[[Vaelith Morryn]]

This is deliberate.

Someone is continuing the effort to frame Vaelith for the conspiracy.

## Actual Thief

**[[Corvin Ashmore]]**

A quiet bureaucratic aide present behind Varn during the speech.

Corvin secretly belongs to the [[Veiled Scoria]].

He realized what the assassins were attempting to recover and stole the ring first.

### Important

Corvin is **not necessarily an enemy**.

He believes the object beneath Drakenshroud must remain sealed.

Finding him gives the party their first knowledgeable source about what actually lies beneath the mountain.

---

# Act IV — The Cult Connection

The [[Pyrebound Synod]] is divided.

## Traditional Synod

Led by [[Arch-Ember Seer Kaelrix]].

They believe the power beneath Drakenshroud is:

**The Heart-Ember of Vyrkael**

They believe it is a sacred remnant of the ancient dragon [[Vyrkael the Ash-Scourged]].

They want it protected until a worthy individual can claim it.

---

## The Ascendant Flame

A radical faction hidden within the Synod.

Their belief:

> "War does not destroy nations. It reveals who deserved to rule them."

They believe Prythian has become weak.

Their goal is to trigger enough conflict to burn away the existing political order.

They have allied themselves with sympathetic Iron Cohort officers.

They helped organize Reth Varn's assassination.

### What They Don't Know

The teachings that created the Ascendant Flame have been subtly influenced by:

[[Kazariel the False Prophet]]

They believe they are following ancient draconic prophecy.

In reality, they are helping prepare Drakenshroud for Kazariel.

---

# Act V — Project Cinderwake

See: [[Project Cinderwake]]

The party eventually discovers a secret alliance between:

- Radical Pyrebound cultists
- Iron Cohort officers
- Industrialists
- Political officials

They have been experimenting with the Aether beneath Drakenshroud.

Their objective:

**Create Aether-enhanced soldiers.**

Their subjects demonstrate:

- Increased strength
- Extreme aggression
- Heightened obedience
- Heat manipulation
- Auditory hallucinations
- Visions
- Strange reactions to Kazariel's Mark

The conspirators believe these are side effects.

They aren't.

---

# Act VI — Beneath Drakenshroud

See: [[Beneath Drakenshroud]]

Following Cinderwake eventually takes the party beneath the mountain.

The descent reveals four historical layers.

1. [[Beneath Drakenshroud#The Emberworks]]
2. [[Beneath Drakenshroud#The Dragon Crypts]]
3. [[Beneath Drakenshroud#The Crucible Below]]
4. [[Beneath Drakenshroud#The Ashen Vault]]

The deeper the party travels, the clearer it becomes that Drakenshroud was constructed over something far older than the city.

---

# Act VII — The Relic

Within the [[Beneath Drakenshroud#The Ashen Vault]] rests:

## The Shard of the Ashen Will

One of the eight fragments connected to [[Kazariel the False Prophet]].

The Pyrebound Synod believes it is draconic.

It isn't.

The fragment predates Drakenshroud.

Ancient carvings surrounding the vault depict an **eight-part symbol**.

The party should immediately recognize its resemblance to Kazariel's mark.

---

# The Trial of Ash

The fragment cannot simply be removed.

A ritual must select its bearer.

The party is eventually brought to or activates the:

**Trial of Ash**

The ritual declares:

> "One flame."
>
> "One vessel."
>
> "One will."
>
> "Two enter."
>
> "One leaves worthy."

Initially, the party may believe they are going to fight champions of the Synod.

The doors open.

Two combatants enter from opposite sides.

**Both are members of the party.**

The Trial demands a fight to the death.

> [!warning]
> The ritual can demand death without mechanically forcing the players to comply. Fighting, cheating the ritual, refusing Kazariel, or attempting to break the Trial can each have different consequences.

---

# Military Fallout

The investigation exposes fractures inside the [[Iron Cohort]].

## Crown Loyalists

Remain loyal to [[King Aldric Valtheris]] and Aetherhaven.

## Cinderwake Officers

Believe military rule is necessary to save Prythian.

[[Colonel Garran Volsk]] is associated with this faction.

## Ashen Reformers

Soldiers horrified by corruption and Cinderwake experimentation.

Potential leader:

[[Commander Darius Voss]]

Voss is respected by [[Hobrin Slate]].

The party can exploit these divisions by:

- Exposing Cinderwake
- Hiding Cinderwake
- Blaming the Crown
- Blaming the Synod
- Arming workers
- Recruiting soldiers
- Blackmailing officers
- Killing commanders
- Forging military orders
- Protecting sympathetic officers

Create enough distrust that:

**Drakenshroud's Iron Cohort refuses orders from Aetherhaven.**

This becomes one of the first major fractures capable of producing civil war within [[Prythian]].

# DM Notes

> [!danger] The Bigger Picture
> Kazariel doesn't necessarily need the party to correctly identify every conspirator.
>
> He needs instability.
>
> Every accusation, riot, assassination, military defection, and broken alliance makes Prythian easier to conquer.

The party should therefore be able to **solve the mystery honestly OR manipulate what they discover for their own purposes**.

Either path advances the campaign.
