# Project Cinderwake

> [!summary]
> A classified program attempting to harness the Aether beneath Drakenshroud to create enhanced soldiers.

## Participants

Cinderwake is supported by individuals within:

- [[Iron Cohort]]
- [[Pyrebound Synod]]
- Drakenshroud's industrial elite
- Drakenshroud's government

Not every member of these organizations knows the project exists.

---

# Objective

Cinderwake's creators believe the Aether beneath Drakenshroud can be used to create soldiers capable of surviving conflicts ordinary troops cannot.

Desired enhancements include:

- Increased strength
- Greater endurance
- Reduced fear response
- Heightened obedience
- Resistance to extreme heat
- Limited Aether manipulation

---

# Test Subjects

Many subjects are drawn from:

- Prisoners
- Deserters
- Political prisoners
- Criminals
- Disappeared laborers

[[Tavian Rusk]] once transported 23 prisoners beneath the mountain.

Only four returned.

---

# Observed Side Effects

Subjects have demonstrated:

- Extreme aggression
- Obsessive loyalty
- Auditory hallucinations
- Recurring dreams
- Heat radiating from their bodies
- Aether burns
- Personality changes
- Attraction toward the Caldera
- Strange reactions to individuals bearing Kazariel's Mark

Common hallucination:

> Chains stretching across an endless black horizon.

Some subjects hear a voice.

It tells them:

> **"Become."**

---

# The Actual Cause

Cinderwake researchers believe they are drawing power from a natural Aether well.

They are partially correct.

The well contains:

[[Shard of the Ashen Will]]

One of the eight fragments associated with [[Kazariel the False Prophet]].

Repeated exposure is making test subjects receptive to Kazariel.

Cinderwake isn't creating perfect soldiers.

It is creating **potential vessels**.
