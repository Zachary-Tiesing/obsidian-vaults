# Opportunity Attacks

When a hostile creature you can see moves out of your reach, you can use your reaction to make one melee attack against it just before it leaves your reach.

Forced movement and movement that does not use the creature’s movement, action, or reaction normally does not provoke. [[Disengage]] prevents opportunity attacks for the turn.
