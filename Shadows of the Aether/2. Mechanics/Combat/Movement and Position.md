# Movement and Position

You can move up to your speed on your turn and split that movement around actions. Difficult terrain costs 1 extra foot of movement for each foot traveled.

Moving through another creature’s space and squeezing are subject to size and hostility restrictions. Standing from [[Prone]] costs movement equal to half your speed.
