# Resting

## Short Rest
A short rest is at least **1 hour** of downtime. A character can spend Hit Dice to regain hit points, adding Constitution modifier to each die spent (minimum 0 hit points regained from each die after modifiers).

## Long Rest
A long rest is at least **8 hours**, with no more than 2 hours of light activity such as reading, talking, eating, or standing watch. At the end, a character regains all lost hit points and regains spent Hit Dice up to half their total (minimum one).

A character cannot benefit from more than one long rest in a 24-hour period and normally must begin the rest with at least 1 hit point.
