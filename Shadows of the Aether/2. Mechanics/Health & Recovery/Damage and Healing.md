# Damage and Healing

Damage reduces current hit points. At 0 hit points, a creature normally falls [[Unconscious]] and begins making [[Death Saving Throws]] unless an effect kills it outright.

## Resistance / Vulnerability / Immunity
- **Resistance:** take half damage of the relevant type.
- **Vulnerability:** take double damage of the relevant type.
- **Immunity:** take no damage of the relevant type.

Apply relevant modifiers to damage before resistance or vulnerability unless a specific rule says otherwise.

## Healing
Healing restores hit points up to the creature’s hit point maximum. Healing has no effect on a dead creature unless the effect specifically restores life.
