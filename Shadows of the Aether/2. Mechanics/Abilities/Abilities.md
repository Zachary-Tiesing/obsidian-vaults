# Abilities

The six abilities describe broad physical and mental capabilities.

- [[Strength]] — physical power
- [[Dexterity]] — agility, reflexes, coordination
- [[Constitution]] — endurance and vitality
- [[Intelligence]] — reasoning, memory, learned knowledge
- [[Wisdom]] — awareness, intuition, practical judgment
- [[Charisma]] — force of personality and social influence
