# Spells

This library catalogs the **319 spells in D&D 5e SRD 5.1**. The spell notes are intentionally lightweight catalog entries so they stay fast to browse and can be expanded with the exact rules text you use at your table.

> [!important] Scope
> This is the **2014 SRD 5.1** spell catalog. It does not yet include non-SRD spells from books such as *Xanathar's Guide to Everything* or *Tasha's Cauldron of Everything*.
