# Healing Potions

Healing potions restore hit points when consumed. In 2014 D&D 5e, drinking a potion or administering one to another creature normally requires an **action**.

|Potion|Rarity|Healing|
|---|---|--:|
|Potion of Healing|Common|`2d4 + 2`|
|Potion of Greater Healing|Uncommon|`4d4 + 4`|
|Potion of Superior Healing|Rare|`8d4 + 8`|
|Potion of Supreme Healing|Very Rare|`10d4 + 20`|

## Average Healing

|Potion|Average HP|
|---|--:|
|Potion of Healing|7 HP|
|Potion of Greater Healing|14 HP|
|Potion of Superior Healing|28 HP|
|Potion of Supreme Healing|45 HP|

## Using a Healing Potion

- **Drink a potion:** Action
- **Administer to another creature:** Action
- Healing potions restore hit points immediately after being consumed.
- A creature cannot be healed above its hit point maximum.
