# Death Saving Throws

At the start of your turn while at 0 hit points, make a death saving throw.

- **10 or higher:** success.
- **9 or lower:** failure.
- **3 successes:** stabilize.
- **3 failures:** die.
- **Natural 1:** counts as two failures.
- **Natural 20:** regain 1 hit point.

Taking damage while at 0 hit points causes a failure; a critical hit causes two failures. Damage equal to or greater than your hit point maximum can kill you outright.
