# Mounted Combat

A willing creature at least one size larger than you, with suitable anatomy, can serve as a mount. A controlled mount shares your initiative and is limited mainly to Dash, Disengage, and Dodge; an independent mount acts on its own initiative.

If an effect moves your mount against its will or knocks it prone, you may need a Dexterity saving throw to remain mounted.
