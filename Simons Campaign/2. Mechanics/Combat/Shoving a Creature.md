# Shoving a Creature

In the 2014 rules, shoving is a special melee attack made as part of the [[Attack]] action against a creature no more than one size larger than you.

Make a Strength (Athletics) check contested by the target’s Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics). On success, either knock the target [[Prone]] or push it 5 ft. away.
