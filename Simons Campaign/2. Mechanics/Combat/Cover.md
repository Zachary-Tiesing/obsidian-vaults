# Cover

Cover can improve AC and Dexterity saving throws when an obstacle blocks part of a target.

| Cover | Benefit |
|---|---|
| Half cover | +2 AC and Dex saves |
| Three-quarters cover | +5 AC and Dex saves |
| Total cover | Cannot be targeted directly by an attack or spell, though some area effects can still reach it |
