# Attack Rolls

To make an attack roll, roll a d20 and add the appropriate ability modifier and proficiency bonus when proficient. If the total equals or exceeds the target’s Armor Class, the attack hits.

A natural 20 on an attack roll is a critical hit; a natural 1 misses regardless of modifiers.
