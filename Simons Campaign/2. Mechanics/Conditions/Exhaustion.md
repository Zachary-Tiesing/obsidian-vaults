# Exhaustion

Exhaustion has six cumulative levels in the 2014 rules.

| Level | Effect |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Disadvantage on ability checks |
| 2 | Speed halved |
| 3 | Disadvantage on attack rolls and saving throws |
| 4 | Hit point maximum halved |
| 5 | Speed reduced to 0 |
| 6 | Death |

Finishing a long rest can reduce exhaustion by one level when the character also has food and drink, subject to the campaign's resting rules.
