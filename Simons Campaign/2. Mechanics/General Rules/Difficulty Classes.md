# Difficulty Classes

A Difficulty Class (DC) represents how hard a task or effect is to resist.

| Difficulty | DC |
|---|---:|
| Very easy | 5 |
| Easy | 10 |
| Medium | 15 |
| Hard | 20 |
| Very hard | 25 |
| Nearly impossible | 30 |

A check succeeds when the total equals or exceeds the DC.
