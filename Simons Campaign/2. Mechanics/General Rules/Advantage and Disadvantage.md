# Advantage and Disadvantage

With advantage, roll two d20s and use the higher result. With disadvantage, roll two d20s and use the lower result.

Multiple sources of advantage do not stack, and multiple sources of disadvantage do not stack. If you have at least one source of each, they cancel and you roll one d20.
