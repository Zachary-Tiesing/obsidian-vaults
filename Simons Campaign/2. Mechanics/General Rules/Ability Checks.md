# Ability Checks

An ability check resolves an uncertain task that is not an attack. Roll **d20 + the relevant ability modifier**, add proficiency if an applicable skill/tool proficiency applies, then compare the result to a DC or opposing check.

**Typical DCs:** 5 very easy, 10 easy, 15 medium, 20 hard, 25 very hard, 30 nearly impossible.
