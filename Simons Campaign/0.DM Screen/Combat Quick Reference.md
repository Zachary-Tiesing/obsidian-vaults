## On a Turn
- Move up to speed — can split movement.
- Take one [[Actions in Combat|action]].
- Take one [[Bonus Actions|bonus action]] only if a rule grants one.
- One simple object interaction is normally free as part of movement/action.
- One [[Reactions|reaction]] per round cycle, regained at the start of your turn.

## Fast Combat Reminders
- Leaving an enemy's reach can trigger [[Opportunity Attacks]].
- [[Prone]] changes melee/ranged attack advantage depending on distance.
- [[Cover]]: +2 / +5 AC and Dex saves for half / three-quarters cover.
- Concentration check after damage: Con save DC 10 or half damage, whichever is higher.
