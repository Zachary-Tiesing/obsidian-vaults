# Temporary Hit Points

Temporary hit points are a separate buffer, not healing. Damage removes temporary hit points before normal hit points.

- Temporary hit points do not stack; when you gain a new amount, choose whether to keep the old amount or replace it.
- Healing does not restore temporary hit points.
- They normally last until depleted or until you finish a long rest unless the granting effect says otherwise.
