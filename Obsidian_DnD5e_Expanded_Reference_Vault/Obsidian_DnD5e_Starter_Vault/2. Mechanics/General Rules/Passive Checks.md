# Passive Checks

A passive check represents an average result for repeated or secret checks. The usual formula is **10 + all modifiers that normally apply to the check**.

For advantage, add 5; for disadvantage, subtract 5. Passive Wisdom (Perception) is commonly used to notice hidden creatures.
