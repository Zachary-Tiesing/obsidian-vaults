# Petrified

- Is transformed, along with carried/worn nonmagical objects, into a solid inanimate substance.
- Is incapacitated, cannot move or speak, and is unaware of surroundings.
- Attacks against it have advantage; it automatically fails Strength and Dexterity saves.
- Has resistance to all damage and is immune to poison and disease while petrified.
