# Concentration

Some spells require concentration. You can concentrate on only one concentration effect at a time. Concentration ends if you start another concentration effect, become incapacitated, die, or fail a concentration check after taking damage.

After taking damage, make a Constitution saving throw with DC 10 or half the damage taken, whichever is higher.
