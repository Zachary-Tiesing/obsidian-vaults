# Grappling

In the 2014 rules, grappling is a special melee attack made as part of the [[Attack]] action. You need a free hand and the target can be no more than one size larger than you.

Make a Strength (Athletics) check contested by the target’s Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics). On success, the target is [[Grappled]].

A grappled creature can use its action to try to escape with the same contest. Moving a grappled creature normally costs extra movement.
